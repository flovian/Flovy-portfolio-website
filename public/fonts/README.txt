Drop the Monsterat font files in this folder and they will be picked up
automatically by `src/styles/fonts.css`. Prefer .woff2 and use these names:

  Monsterat-Regular.woff2     (400 — body text)
  Monsterat-Medium.woff2      (500)
  Monsterat-SemiBold.woff2    (600 — headings)
  Monsterat-Bold.woff2        (700)

If your files have different names, update the `src:` paths in
`src/styles/fonts.css`. Until the files are added, the site falls back to
Poppins + system fonts.

Professional portrait
---------------------
Add your headshot here as `flovian.jpg` (portrait orientation, ideally
1200x1500 or larger). It is used in the hero and the About section. Until it
exists, both show a designed typographic placeholder.
